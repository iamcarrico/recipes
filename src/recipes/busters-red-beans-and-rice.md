---
title: Buster's Red Beans and Rice
description: The Buster's of New Orleans recipe, made by the potful.
tags:
  - Dinner
servings: Serves 15 to 18
cookTime: 3 to 4 hours
source: Buster's of New Orleans
created: '2026-10-25'
ingredients:
  - '**Beans**'
  - 8 cans red kidney beans
  - 1 large yellow onion (or 2 medium), finely diced
  - 1 bunch green onions, finely sliced
  - 6 stalks celery (about half a bunch), leafy tops and ends trimmed, finely diced
  - 4 dried bay leaves
  - 1 stick butter
  - 3 Louisiana hot link sausages, sliced into thin coins
  - 3/4 ham steak, cut into small cubes
  - 2 to 3 tablespoons salt and pepper
  - Hot sauce, to taste (optional)
  - '**Rice**'
  - 4 cups water
  - 1/2 stick butter
  - 2 tablespoons salt
  - 2 cups rice
directions:
  - '**Beans**'
  - Put the beans in a large pot (8 quarts) over high heat.
  - Add the onion, green onions, celery, bay leaves, butter, sausage, ham, and salt and pepper as you cut them up, stirring the whole time so nothing burns on the high heat. The sausage and ham are already cooked, so they go straight in.
  - Taste before adding any hot sauce. The hot links make the beans spicy on their own; with a milder sausage, hot sauce is recommended. Add a little at a time and taste after stirring it in.
  - Turn the heat down as low as it goes and simmer uncovered for 3 to 4 hours, stirring often. Leave the lid off so the water can steam away. The longer it simmers, the better it tastes.
  - '**Rice**'
  - Start the rice when the beans have been cooking for about 3 1/2 hours.
  - Bring the water, butter and salt to a boil in a pot.
  - Add the rice and turn the heat down to a simmer. Do not stir it again. Cover the pot, leaving the lid slightly open at the side so steam can escape.
  - After 20 minutes, take the lid off and dig a fork down to the bottom of the pot. If there's still water under the rice, let it simmer a little longer. Take it off the heat as soon as the water is gone, or it will start to burn.
  - Serve the beans over the rice.
---

This is one pot of beans. For a crowd of 40 to 60, make four pots, and a couple more pots of rice.

Shopping for a crowd: Costco sells red kidney beans in 8-can packs (don't grab the black beans next to them by mistake), Louisiana hot links, and ham steaks in packs of 3. One pack of 8 cans makes one pot. Green onions and bay leaves will likely need a regular grocery store. An electric can opener helps.

A rice cooker works fine if you'd rather; skip the rice steps above.
