---
title: Blueberry Muffins
tags:
  - Breakfast
servings: 12 muffins
difficulty: Easy
rating: 5
source: Stacy Miller
created: '2018-01-04'
ingredients:
  - '**Muffins:**'
  - 1 egg
  - ¾ c milk
  - ½ c canola oil
  - 2c flour
  - ½ c sugar
  - 1T baking powder
  - 1t salt
  - ¾ c blueberries
  - '**Topping:**'
  - ¼ c brown sugar
  - ½ t cinnamon
  - ¼ c chopped nuts (optional)
directions:
  - Preheat oven to 400 and line pan with muffin cups
  - Beat egg, stir in milk and oil
  - Mix dry ingredients and add to liquid mixture
  - Stir in blueberries
  - Fill muffin cups to ¾ full
  - Sprinkle topping
  - Bake for 20 minutes
---
