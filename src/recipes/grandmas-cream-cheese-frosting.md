---
title: Grandma's Cream Cheese Frosting
tags:
  - Dessert
source: Grandma Carrico
created: '2018-01-12'
ingredients:
  - 1/4 cup softened butter
  - 4 ounces softened cream cheese
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 1 3/4 cups powdered sugar
directions:
  - Mix together butter and cream cheese.
  - Add vanilla then sugar.
  - Beat well.
---

I usually 4x this recipe for an 8" round cake.
