ingredients:
  - 1 c Sugar
  - 3 T Water
  - 1/8 tsp. Cream of Tartar
  - 4 T Butter (4 pieces)
  - 1/2 c heavy cream
  - 1 tsp kosher salt
directions:
  - Prepare all ingredients _before_ starting.
  - Mix sugar, water, and cream of tartar in the bottom of a high-sided, heavy bottom saucepan.
  - Heat on medium heat, stirring to dissolve the sugar. Continue boiling, stirring.
  - _This is where the process speeds up_
  - It will take some time to go from boiling to cooking. But once it does, it moves fast. Once you have a light amber color, turn to low and keep stirring.
  - Just before the caramel burns, incorporate the butter. Then slowly mix in the heavy cream. Finally, toss in that salt.
---

Leftover caramel lasts for about a month in the fridge.
