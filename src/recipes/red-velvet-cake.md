---
title: Red Velvet Cake
tags:
  - Dessert
source: Stacey Miller
created: '2019-04-21'
ingredients:
  - '**Cake:**'
  - 1 1/2 c sugar
  - 1 c buttermilk
  - 1 1/2 c vegetable oil
  - 2 tsp vanilla
  - 1 tsp vinegar
  - 2 large eggs
  - 2 1/2 c self-rising flour
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - 1 tsp cocoa
  - 1 bottle red food coloring
  - '**Frosting:**'
  - 5 T flour
  - 1 c milk
  - 1 stick butter
  - 1/2 c crisco
  - 1 c sugar
  - 2 tsp vanilla
directions:
  - Prepare 3 9” round cake pans. Heat oven to 350.
  - Mix sugar, buttermilk, vegetable oil, vanilla, vinegar well. Beat in two large eggs.
  - In a separate bowl, mix together self-rising flour, baking soda, and cocoa. Add to creamed mixture.
  - Beat well with electric mixer. Add 1 bottle red food coloring. Mix well.
  - Pour batter evenly, bake 20 minutes. Cool for 10 minutes, then allow to cool completely on wire racks.
  - '**Frosting**'
  - Mix flour and milk in a Tupperware, shake well. Heat over medium heat until thick then let cool.
  - Cream together butter, crisco, sugar, and vanilla. Add in thickened flour mixture and beat until fluffy (~15 minutes)
