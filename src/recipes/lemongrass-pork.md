---
title: Lemongrass Pork
tags: []
source: Jasmine Vu
created: '2018-01-04'
ingredients:
  - 1 lb. Pork butt — thinly sliced
  - Oyster sauce (enough to coat pork; Panda brand okay)
  - Minced garlic
  - Salt + pepper
  - ~1 T soy sauce
  - ~2 T honey
  - Lemongrass (chopped/frozen, 5T – a lot)
  - ~1 T sesame oil
directions:
  - Marinate at least 1 hr, best if overnight
  - Grill or cook in cast iron skillet
  - Serve over rice (w/ nuoc mam) or vermicelli or salad
---
