---
title: Sweet Potato and Carrot Puree
tags:
  - Side
  - Thanksgiving
source: (Tony Lance) Adapted from Silver Palate Cookbook By Julee Rosso and Sheila Lukins
created: '2025-11-22'
ingredients:
  - 4 large sweet potatoes (about 2 pounds)
  - 1 pound carrots
  - 2 ½ cups water
  - 1 ½ tsp. granulated sugar
  - 3 ½ Tbsp. sweet butter, softened
  - ¼ tsp. salt and a pinch of cayenne
  - ¼ cup crème fraiche
  - ½ tsp. freshly grated nutmeg
directions:
  - Preheat oven to 400°.
  - Scrub potatoes and dry. Place them in a large iron skillet. (12 inch) Set on the center rack of the preheated oven and bake for about 1 hour, or until the potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork. Prick them with a poultry skewer after 30 minutes.
  - Meanwhile, peel and trim the carrots (if organic, peel lightly) and slant cut them into 1-inch lengths. Put them in a saucepan and add the water, sugar, 2 Tbsp. of the butter, and salt and pepper to taste. Set over medium heat, bring to a boil, and cook uncovered until the water has evaporated and the carrots begin to sizzle in the butter, about 30 minutes. The carrots should be tender. If not, add a little additional water and cook until the carrots are done and all the liquid has evaporated.
  - Scrape out the flesh of the sweet potatoes and combine with the carrots in the bowl of a food processor fitted with a steel blade. Add the remaining 1 ½ Tbsp. butter and crème fraiche and process until very smooth. Process by pulsing on an off.
  - Add nutmeg and season with salt and cayenne. Process briefly to blend.
  - To reheat, transfer to an ovenproof greased serving dish and cover with foil. Heat in a preheated 350° oven for about 15-20 minutes, or until hot.
  - This dish can be prepared 1-2 days ahead.
