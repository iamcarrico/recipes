---
title: Grandma's Carrot Cake
tags:
  - Dessert
source: Grandma Carrico
created: '2018-01-12'
ingredients:
  - 5 eggs
  - 2 1/2 cups sugar
  - 1 7/8 cups oil
  - 2 1/2 cups flour
  - 2 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
  - 2 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon
  - 1/2 teaspoon ginger
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  - 1/4 teaspoon allspice
  - 1/2 cup pineapple crushed
  - 2/3 teaspoon salt
  - 4 cups grated carrot (10 oz)
directions:
  - Preheat oven to 350.
  - Beat eggs, add sugar and oil. Beat well.
  - Combine dry ingredients together and add to egg mixture.
  - Drain pineapple juice, then add pineapple and grated carrots. Beat well.
  - Grease flour and parchment paper 3 - 8" pans. Divide batter between pans.
  - Bake at 350' for 30 minutes
