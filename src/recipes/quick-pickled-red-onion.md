title: Less Quick Pickled Red Onion
tags:
  - Pantry
servings: 2 cups (with pickling liquid)
prepTime: 5 min
cookTime: 45 min
totalTime: 50 min

source: cooking.nytimes.com

created: '2023-11-05'
ingredients:
  - 1 large red onion
  - 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  - 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
  - 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  - 3/4 cup vinegar (white or red wine, distilled or apple cider)
directions:
  - Thinly slice the red onion by halving through the root and slicing in the same direction. Separate the slices by hand, as best you can, without getting too obsessive. Place the onion slices in a heat-proof, wide-mouth jar.
  - Add peppercorns, and any other aromatics, to the jar
  - Add the salt, sugar, vinegar and ¼ cup water to a small saucepan. Heat over medium-high, stirring to dissolve the salt and sugar. As soon as the mixture comes to a boil, remove from the heat and pour half of the liquid over the onion, pressing down the slices with the back of a spoon. Add the remaining liquid, continuing to press down the slices.
  - Cover and give the jar a gentle shake. Put in the fridge for a day, and up to two weeks
