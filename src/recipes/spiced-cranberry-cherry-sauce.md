---
title: Spiced Cranberry-Cherry Sauce
tags:
  - Thanksgiving 2025
source: Claude
created: '2025-11-22'
ingredients:
  - 1 (12 oz) bag fresh cranberries
  - 3/4 cup sugar
  - 3/4 cup fresh orange juice (about 2 oranges)
  - 1/4 cup water
  - Zest of 1 orange
  - 1/2 cup dried cherries (preferably tart), roughly chopped
  - 1 cinnamon stick
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
  - 2 tablespoons bourbon (optional but recommended)
  - Pinch of salt
directions:
  - In a medium saucepan, combine orange juice, water, sugar, orange zest, dried cherries, cinnamon stick, allspice, and salt.
  - Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring to dissolve sugar.
  - Add cranberries and return to a boil.
  - Reduce heat to medium and simmer 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally. Cranberries will pop and sauce will thicken.
  - Remove from heat and discard cinnamon stick.
  - Stir in bourbon if using.
  - Let cool to room temperature (it will thicken more as it cools).
  - Can be made 3-4 days ahead and refrigerated.
---

Tip: The dried cherries add wonderful texture and a deeper, more complex fruit flavor. The allspice and cinnamon bring warm spice notes, and the bourbon adds richness without being boozy once cooked!
